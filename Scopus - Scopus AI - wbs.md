Synthesis and Future Directions
Theory-based whistleblowing is a multi-faceted approach that integrates individual ethical considerations,
organizational culture, leadership dynamics, and digital innovations to enhance fraud risk management.
Empirical evidence underscores the importance of combining micro-level deviance theories with macro
frameworks such as the Three Lines Model and digital advancements. Despite strong support for integrated
whistleblowing systems, gaps remain in standardizing frameworks, particularly in culturally diverse and
emerging markets.
